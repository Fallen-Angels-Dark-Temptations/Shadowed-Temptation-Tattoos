"Freelance Inkwork for the Haunted, the Bold, and the Beautifully Broken."


Hey, friend. I'm Angel — the founder, artist, and soul behind Shadowed Temptation Tattoos™. This is a freelance tattoo vnture born from obsession: with storytelling, with symbolism, and with the raw beauty of the gothic and alternative scene.

I specialize in illustrative tattoos that blend dark fantasy, horror, and emotional resonance. Every piece is a collaboration — your scars, your shadows, your dreams — woven into ink that speaks louder than words.

This isn't a corporate shop. It's personal. It's intimate. It's for those who feel more alive in the moonlight than the sunshine. If you're looking for a tattoo that feels like a secret spell or a haunted heirloom, you're in the right place.
